# 02 — Phạm vi MVP

## Trong phạm vi MVP
- **Auth/RBAC**: đăng nhập, user/role/unit/entity scope, audit.
- **Organization/Researchers**: cấu trúc tổ chức, hồ sơ nhà nghiên cứu, expertise, identifiers.
- **Funding calls**: program/call, form/checklist, publish.
- **Proposals**: draft, team, workplan, budget, documents, validate/submit.
- **Review/Council**: reviewer pool, COI, assignment, scorecard, council, decision.
- **Projects**: activation, baseline, milestones, progress, change, deliverables, closure cơ bản.
- **Outputs**: publication/output CRUD, DOI, authors, duplicate/verification.
- **Documents**: storage abstraction, versions, scan hooks, extraction pipeline, search.
- **AI MVP**: document extraction, summary, proposal completeness/consistency, reviewer suggestion, RAG, metadata extraction.
- **Workflow/Notifications**: inbox, approvals, deadlines, email/in-app.
- **Dashboard/Reports**: portfolio, progress, outputs, operational SLA, annual report draft.
- **Deployment**: Docker Compose, CI, Cloud Build, Cloud Run, Cloud SQL, GCS, Secret Manager.

## Ngoài phạm vi giai đoạn đầu
- Finance integration sâu & settlement automation (chỉ snapshot/import ở MVP).
- Ethics/Integrity đầy đủ, IP/technology transfer & reward workflow nâng cao.
- Public/partner portal & mobile/PWA nâng cao.
- BigQuery/Data Warehouse, predictive portfolio models.
- Multi-institution consortium, qualified e-signature, legal archiving chuyên dụng.
- Thay thế kế toán/ERP; chỉ nhận & đối soát dữ liệu tài chính được phép.
- Tự động ra quyết định tài trợ/đạo đức/nghiệm thu/khen thưởng/SHTT.
